Frequently Asked Questions

Can my child learn Marathi online from outside India?

Yes. Classes are live on Zoom or Google Meet, so students can join from the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, Singapore or any other country.

My child has never learned Marathi. Is that okay?

Yes. The Marathi for Beginners course starts with the alphabet and pronunciation and is suitable for kids and adults.

Do you teach adults?

Yes. Marathi for Beginners is open to both kids and adults.

Is there a free demo class?

Yes. Book a free demo class through WhatsApp or the enquiry form.

What are the fees?

Fees are shared on enquiry, in INR for India and in your currency for international students.
